How to play

Each of the four teams takes one action per round, plus one optional extra action during the game. Select CENTRAL, WEST, SOUTH, EAST, or NORTH before choosing your action. Teams can build on every island.

  1. Extra move. Once per game, each team may take a second action immediately after its first move, before handing off or ending the round. Select “+1.” The extra move is used only when an action is completed; you can keep it for later while choosing.
  2. Connect. An empty harbor circle is inactive; its center dot appears when activated. Build a shared port on any outer island, then launch an L1 ship serving that island. Upgrade it on later turns. L1 / L2 / L3 transport 10 / 20 / 30 people per round.
  3. Build. Develop each island in order: plot 1, then 2, through 6. Each square holds nine housing units or one entertainment venue; either unlocks the next plot for any team. Choose the income level of each housing unit. The team that builds the housing units owns them, wherever they are located. The team signal flags sit outside CENTRAL. Click a flag to highlight its ownership lines and housing units. All teams share CENTRAL. Empty units are outlined; filled units are solid with a check.
  4. Create jobs. Small / medium / large industries create 25 / 50 / 100 workers. Each size has six business card options based on workforce profiles from Q. The mixes favor $ and $$ workers. CENTRAL has four slots for each size: inner ring small, middle ring medium, outer ring large. Empty slots are outlined; businesses fill them black, clockwise from the top. Click CENTRAL to see waiting workers and remaining slots.
  5. Welcome workers. Ports, ships, and entertainment are shared infrastructure. After all four turns, workers need a port, a ship with space, and a housing unit at their exact income level, regardless of which team owns it. They prefer more entertainment, then the plot furthest from the port: 6, 5, 4, 3, 2, 1. Remaining ties favor a different island each round: NORTH, EAST, SOUTH, WEST. Workers queue in creation order.
  6. Score. Only your own housing units contribute to your team’s score, across all islands. With recurring rent off, a filled housing unit earns its income value once. With recurring rent on, every filled housing unit pays its income value at the end of each round, after arrivals, including the final round. Rent accumulates: a $$$$ home occupied for three round endings earns $12. In both modes, empty homes subtract their value while vacant; this penalty does not accumulate. Industry, ports, ships, and entertainment do not earn points.

↓ saves a game file; ↑ loads it. Saves include team controls, recurring rent, collected rent, the current turn and unused extra moves. Loading a save made during arrivals replays that movement and pays that round’s rent once.

Choose You or AI for each team and turn recurring rent on or off before starting. The moving outline marks the active team on the board and in the scores at the top. AI teams follow the same rules and take their turns automatically. Pause AI to review play; undo also pauses it. Loaded practice games wait for you to resume AI.

Workers without a suitable housing unit stay in the center for the next round. The highest score after the final round wins. Equal scores share the win.
